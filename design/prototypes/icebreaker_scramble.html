Icebreaker · jhale@SEC-CAM-02

Password strength Medium · base 12 s · deterministic

Adds trace while it runs (4 %/s x your skill's trace gain) to every tracer watching SEC-CAM-02.

The effect: while the Icebreaker runs, the next letter to find cycles through random characters; when its share of the progress is reached it locks on the real letter (with a short flash), and the scramble moves to the next cell. Letters further on stay as dots. When you stop it (or the connection drops), the scramble stops and the cell shows a dot again; Resume picks up where it was.

Unchanged: the timing (letters lock at progress × length, deterministic), the progress bar, the buttons and the assists. The effect is display only; the cycled characters are drawn from the same character set as the password (letters, digits, symbols) and never show the right one by chance.
