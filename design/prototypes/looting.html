Bodies and containers use the same screen. E on a downed character or a container opens it (holding E on a body carries it instead). Items keep their footprints; a drop shows green where it fits and red where it does not. Ammo merges into your stacks. You can also plant items on a body or in a container.

Keychain: keys (brass key icon) and keycards (blue card icon) now share the keychain; neither takes grid space or can be dropped. Their keychain moves to yours with a click; Take all takes it too.
