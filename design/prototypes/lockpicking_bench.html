A standalone prototype of the M10 pin-tumbler minigame (ADR 0073). Nothing here touches the game code.

Standard lock

Pick this lock

Roll the mouse wheel up for tension. The mouse moves the pick tip: slide it under a pin while it is down, then lift until the pin clicks. Too little tension and set pins drop; too much and they jam or the pick snaps.
