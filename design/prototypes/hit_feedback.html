Hit feedback · M13b

Hit markers: four short ticks around the crosshair for 0.15 s on a body hit (white), slightly longer and yellow on a headshot, a red X held 0.35 s on a kill. A soft tick sound with each. No damage numbers.

Tracers: every third round of the assault rifle and the submachine gun, and every guard shot, as a short bright streak travelling at bullet speed (it reads where fire comes from). Impacts: a hole plus a puff by material (metal sparks, concrete dust, wood splinters, glass cracks), capped and fading; a small dark-red puff on bodies; a blue-white spark on stun hits. Getting hit: the view kicks and the crosshair opens for ~0.3 s.
