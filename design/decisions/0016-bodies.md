# 16. Bodies: carry, discovery, waking by others

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Body handling is a core stealth interaction.

## Decision

Bodies (dead or unconscious) can be carried and dropped, are discovered by guards, and unconscious guards can be woken by other guards but never wake on their own in v0.

## Alternatives considered

- Self-waking unconscious guards: not in v0.

## Consequences

- Knockouts are permanent unless another guard intervenes.
- See [perception and AI](../game/perception-and-ai.md).
