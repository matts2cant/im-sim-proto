# 131. Grenades, mines and their effects (M14)

Status: Accepted (starts after M13c is merged and playtested)

Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

The roadmap's M14 asks for explosives on the existing explosion and damage systems, and the jamming grenade promised by [ADR 0112](0112-hacking-reconciled-with-the-built-game.md#amendment-switches-routers-and-access-points-2026-11-11). Today the `Explosive` component makes barrels blow up (explosive damage, fire, a blast push, noise, chains); the player can throw carried props; a stun damage type exists (the baton); guards do not react to danger (the AI overhaul is M16). The user and the chief engineer settled M14 in a design review (grilling, three rounds).

## Decision

The full specification is [explosives.md](../game/explosives.md). In short:

- **Four grenades**: frag, gas, EMP, jamming; 1x1 items stacking to 5, on the belt, equipped like weapons. Models: Kenney `grenade-a` (frag) and `grenade-b` with its yellow part recoloured green / blue / cyan (gas / EMP / jamming), copied from the user's local Blaster Kit download.
- **One control**: LMB sticks the grenade where a ghost shows it when a surface (wall, floor, ceiling, door, prop) is within 1.2 m, else throws it in a fixed arc (no preview); a stuck grenade follows what it is stuck to; a thrown one bounces (off characters too) and goes off 2.5 s after the throw.
- **Every stuck grenade is a proximity mine**: arms after 2 s, blinks red, triggers on any character in line of sight within 2.5 m (1 m crouched), the player included; crouched at 1 to 2 m, E disarms it with Demolition Trained or better, else it goes off after a 0.5 s beep.
- **Effects** (first guesses, measured by tests): frag kills within 3 m, hurts to 6 m, pushes, chains, no fire; gas, a 5 m cloud for 12 s knocking out anyone inside in about 3 s; EMP, electronics off for 20 s within 6 m (cameras, turrets, lasers, door controllers, lights, the deck) and half the bioenergy drained; jamming, every wireless link within 10 m dropped for 20 s (the deck's Wi-Fi included, guards' radios silent), silent, with a "Jammed" status (blinking amber) on the frame, the Map and the lights.
- **Grenades are Explosive and Breakable**: shot or caught in a blast, they go off, so they chain.
- **Demolition skill** (new, four tiers): throw distance 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 m, disarming from Trained, mines highlighted within 8 m from Advanced, the fuse time on the HUD at Master; blast strength does not scale.
- **Guards** do not react to grenades until M16; they only hear the noises (bounce, blast, hiss, pop; the jammer is silent).
- **Testing**: the shooting range gets a grenade lane, a mine corridor and an EMP/jamming corner; the warehouse gets 2 frag, 1 gas, 1 EMP, 1 jamming as loot.
- **Build**: grenades, throwing, mines and the skill, in parallel with the four effects and the "Jammed" status; then the range and the warehouse.

## Alternatives considered

- **Remote charges**: later.
- **A separate mine item**: one item with two uses gives gas, EMP and jamming mines for free.
- **Hold to charge a throw, or an arc preview**: Deus Ex simplicity first; a preview can follow the playtest.
- **Impact fuses**: a fuse lets grenades bounce round corners and gives time to react.
- **Mines that spare their owner or allies**: needs factions (M15).
- **Mines that cannot be picked up, or that anyone can pick up**: disarming is what the Demolition skill is for.
- **Guards fleeing grenades now**: deferred to the AI overhaul with grenade throwing.
- **Fire from frag grenades**: barrels burn because they hold fuel; a frag does not.
- **Grenades sticking to people**: reads as a joke in a stealth sim.
- **A new gallery room**: the shooting range sits next to the weapons and needs no new hub space.

## Consequences

- New: grenade items, a sticking/ghost and fuse component, a proximity trigger component usable by any explosive, gas cloud, EMP and jammer effect components, the "Jammed" network state, the Demolition skill ([abilities.md](../game/abilities.md)).
- `NetworkState` and the frame, the Map and StatusLight learn the jammed state; ADR 0112's jamming numbers are kept.
- The shooting range and the warehouse are extended; their layouts are measured before placing.

## As built (M14b: the four effects)

**The payload contract** (shared with M14a's grenade entities). A grenade is an entity with an `Explosive` and a `Breakable` (`break_on_depleted` off); `Explosive.explode(cause)` is the one detonation entry. Its effect is a **payload scene** instanced as a child of the entity: `objects/explosives/payloads/{frag,gas,emp,jamming}_payload.tscn`, each an `ExplosivePayload` holding the type's `ExplosiveConfig` (`*_explosive.tres`: the blast) and, for gas and jamming, a `lasting` scene. `Explosive` takes its config from the payload in `_ready` when it has none (the payload may come before or after it among the children) and `ExplosivePayload._ready` calls `Explosive.use_config()` when the Explosive was ready first (the heat is kept). On `Explosive.exploded`, emitted before the entity is freed (with `Explosive.trigger`, the stimulus that set it off), the payload emits `released(origin, cause)` for its effect components (the EMP's `EmpPulse` child) and spawns `lasting` under the level root (`ExplosivePayload.level_of()`: the current scene, or the fixture's topmost node), never under the grenade or what it is stuck to. Whatever sets it off (fuse, mine trigger, shot, blast chain), the release is the same. A blast stimulus whose radius is 0 is not sent (the frag has no fire, the jammer no noise at all); `ExplosiveConfig.fireball` off leaves only the flash and the sound.

- **Frag** (`frag_explosive.tres`): `damage.explosive` 550 over 6.5 m (linear falloff, line of sight), `force.blast` 90 N s over 6 m, no fire, noise 70 m, Kenney `explosionCrunch_000`. Measured (grenade 0.1 m above the floor, horizontal distance to the feet; the damage reaches the receiver, 1.2 m up for the player's body, 1.6 m for a guard's): 2 m kills both (torso -143 / -135), 3 m still kills (-112 of 100 / -107 of 80), 4.5 m leaves both alive with more than half the torso gone (player 100 -> 37, guard 80 -> 20), 7 m nothing. With a linear falloff "more than half" stops at about 5 m (5.0 m for the player, 5.3 m for a guard); from there to 6.5 m the wounds are light (about -13 at 6 m). Barrels at 4 m take 211 (25 health: they go off), other grenades chain the same way. Tested on bodies with the player's and the guards' profiles and on the shipped guard.
- **Gas** (`gas_cloud.tres`, `GasCloud`): 2.5 m radius (5 m across), 12 s; every 0.5 s a uniform `damage.stun.gas` 10 (line of sight to the centre, which rises 1 m, less under a low ceiling); the first pulse one interval after the release. Knockout threshold 60: unconscious after **3.03 s** inside (measured: player body, guard body and shipped guard alike). The stimulus has no source (the cloud is no attacker, so no "from behind" x10 on unaware guards); `damage.stun.gas` causes no hit reaction (`HitReactionConfig.ignored_types`). Drifts 0.1 m/s in a random direction and stops for good 1 m short of a wall; swells in 0.8 s and fades over its last 2 s. Looks: ten soft puffs (`gas_cloud.gdshader`: edges and wall contacts fade; inside it tints the view). Hiss: noise 10 m.
- **EMP** (`emp.tres`, `EmpPulse`): an `emp` stimulus, uniform, STRAIGHT (walls do not stop it), 6 m, intensity = 20 s. **Being electronic is a component, not a tag**: `Powered` and `Device` (and `Energy`, `DeckSession`) call `EmpEffect.listen()` in `_ready`, which adds the shared `disrupted_by_emp` receptron to the entity's StimulusReceiver (creating one when it has none), so no scene lists it; a crate has neither and is untouched. Its `EmpEffect` calls `take_emp(seconds, cause)` on the entity's children in group `emp_reactive`: `Powered` starts an **outage** (`is_on()` = circuit `on` and no outage; it ends by itself; a circuit switched off meanwhile stays off), a `Device` without a Powered (the shipped door controller) runs its own outage, so `state.unpowered` and every existing power rule follow (cameras blind, turrets and lasers stopped, lights hidden, fail-safe doors unlock, fail-secure stay locked, the frame says "No power"), and `DeckSession` unplugs (`emp`). Its second effect, `ChangeAttributeEffect` with the new `of_current` -0.5, halves `energy` (80 -> 40 measured). No damage. Pop: noise 12 m, `zapThreeToneDown`, a small flash. Measured: off at 10 s, back by 25 s.
- **Jamming** (`jamming_field.tres`, `JammingField`): 10 m, 20 s, silent, a small blinking cyan beacon. A wireless link with **either end** inside a running field drops (`JammingField.jams_link()`; fields register in a static list): `DeviceNetwork.is_jammed(device)` (its radio link holds by range but is jammed) leaves the link out of the live reach graph (incidents reach no one, the deck cannot reach it) while `wireless_link()` stays the physical link (subnet, the Map's `physical_network()`, port kinds); the deck's Wi-Fi drops (`DeckSession` unplugs `jammed`, cannot join, `WirelessTool` has no signal); radio messages are lost when the receiver or the sender stands in a field (`AwarenessEffect.radio`, set on `heard_dispatch` and `heard_radio`; logged `radio.jammed`). Cables are unaffected (a deck on a jack inside a field keeps working). **"Jammed"** is a network state (`StatusLight.State.JAMMED`, `NetworkState.state_of()` after disabled): amber blinking on the light and lamp, "Jammed" on the frame, a blinking square and the word "jammed" on the deck's Map (the device stays drawn). Starting, ending and loading a field refresh every status light (`StatusLight.refresh_all()`).
- **Framework**: `Stimulus.uniform` (RADIUS without falloff: the gas dose, the EMP); `ChangeAttributeEffect.of_current`.
- **Saves**: a gas cloud (age, drift, place) and a jamming field (age, place) are spawned saveables; `Powered` and `Device` save their `outage`, so EMPed devices come back at the right time after a load.
- **Debug**: the Spawn tab's Gas, EMP and Jam buttons act at the crosshair.
- **Tests** (`tests/objects/explosives/`): `frag_payload_test`, `gas_cloud_test`, `emp_payload_test`, `jamming_field_test`, `explosive_payload_test` (the contract), on stand-ins built from components (`explosive_fixtures.gd`).

## As built (M14a)

Grenades, throwing, sticking, mines, disarming, the Demolition skill and the HUD parts. The effects (payloads, gas cloud, EMP, jamming field, "Jammed") are M14b's.

- **A grenade is a thrown weapon.** `WeaponData.Kind.THROWN` (new): the item is an EQUIP_WEAPON stack of 5 drawn through the WeaponHolder like any weapon (its model is the view model, it swings like the baton); the trigger hands it to the user's **`Thrower`** component (`items/grenades/thrower.gd`), and `PlayerInventory` takes one from the stack on the holder's `fired` signal, keeping the next in hand. `WeaponData.thrown_scene` (a path: the entity references the item) is the entity spawned. A character without a Thrower cannot use a thrown weapon (guards until M16). HUD: the weapon corner shows the stack's count ("x3"); `is_ranged()` now decides the reticle and its target colour, so melee and thrown weapons both show the plain dot.
- **Thrower** (any character with a WeaponHolder; the player's in `player.tscn`): one ray from the eye up to `stick_reach` 1.2 m on world, objects and the character layers; a surface that is not a character (layer or BodyParts) gives a stick pose (`ThrowRules.stick_transform()`, `Sticky.pose_on()`; changed after the M14 playtest, below) and the entity is stuck on the **collision shape hit** (a sliding door moves its Panel shape, not its body). Otherwise it is thrown: `ThrowRules.launch_velocity()` raises the view by `lift_deg` 20 and solves the launch speed that lands the user's `throw_distance` attribute (base 12 m in `throw_config.tres`) on flat ground from the eye height when looking level; the same speed for every pitch (a fixed arc). It starts 0.35 m ahead of the eye with some spin and ignores its thrower's body for 0.3 s. With `show_ghost` (the player) the drawn weapon's view scene is shown 60 % opaque at the stick pose.
- **Entity** (`items/grenades/grenade.tscn`, inherited by `frag/`, `gas/`, `emp/`, `jamming/<type>_grenade.tscn`): a PhysicsObject (0.4 kg, no linear damping, a bouncy metal material) with Tags, health 10, `take_damage` and `heat_from_fire` receptrons, `Explosive`, `Breakable` (break_on_depleted off), `ImpactNoise` and four new components, each usable on any entity: **`Fuse`** (lit: `Explosive.explode()` after `time` 2.5 s; while lit the body also collides with the character layers, so a thrown grenade bounces off people; saved), **`Sticky`** (freezes the body kinematic and follows the node it was stuck to at the same relative pose every tick, never colliding with its body; lets go and falls when it is freed; saved by path from the entity's parent), **`ProximityTrigger`** (arms `arm_time` 2 s after Sticky's `stuck`; then blinks and every 4 ticks queries a 2.5 m sphere on the character layers, a 1 m one for crouched (`stance.crouch`) candidates, and casts one ray per candidate to its shape's centre on world and objects; the first seen sets `Explosive.explode()` off; saved) and **`MineDisarm`** (a Usable available while armed: see below). Each type scene adds its model, its `display_name` and its **`Payload`**: M14b's `objects/explosives/payloads/<type>_payload.tscn`, which hands the blast config to the Explosive (the entity's Explosive has none of its own; the stand-in config used before the merge is gone). The tests' minimal grenade (`tests/items/grenades/fixtures/test_grenade.tscn`, a plain RigidBody3D box without PhysicsObject) has its own small one.
- **Distances** are gaps from the mine's centre to the character's collision shape (the sphere query measures them), not centre to centre: a standing player 2.0 m away (centre, level) is 1.65 m from the mine. "Walking or running" is read as "not crouched": a character standing still in range triggers it too.
- **Disarming** (`MineDisarm`, E through the Interactor's Usable path): crouched, 1 to 2 m (gap, as above), `mine_disarm` attribute at least 1 (Demolition Trained+): the entity's item goes into the user's PlayerInventory and the entity is removed (no room: nothing happens, "No room in inventory"). Anything else beeps (a generated tone) and lights the entity's Fuse for 0.5 s. EventLog `mine.disarmed`, `mine.disarm_failed {reason: standing, too_close, too_far, untrained}`.
- **Frame**: a component hook, not a class check. `TargetStatus.STATUS_GROUP` (`frame_status`): any component in it answers `frame_word()`; its word comes before the others. ProximityTrigger says the new `Word.ARMED` ("Armed", blinking red) while armed. The name comes from the entity's `display_name` ("Frag grenade").
- **Demolition** (`skill_table.tres`, ADD effects): `throw_distance` +0 / 2 / 4 / 6 m, `mine_disarm` 0 / 1 / 1 / 1, `mine_sense_range` 0 / 0 / 8 / 8 m, `fuse_readout` 0 / 0 / 0 / 1. **`MineSense`** (the player) highlights armed mines within `mine_sense_range` every 10 ticks: a red translucent sphere, unlit, unfogged, drawn after every other transparent thing (over smoke), but depth tested (walls hide it: no seeing mines through walls). The HUD's `Fuse` label shows "Fuse 1.8 s" under the crosshair for the Thrower's last throw while `fuse_readout` is on.
- **Models** (deviation): the brief had the files swapped. The only Kenney grenade with a yellow part is `grenade-a`, the canister, so the frag is the round `grenade-b` and gas, EMP and jamming are `grenade-a` with its yellow caps recoloured green, blue and cyan by `grenade_tint.gdshader` (a material override sampling the colormap: texels inside the yellow swatch take the tint). The GLBs are not edited.
- **Measured**: the throw lands (first touch-down) 11.8 / 13.8 / 15.7 / 17.7 m away per tier (`thrower_test.gd`).
- **Across M14a and M14b** (`tests/scenarios/grenade_effects_test.gd`): a thrown gas grenade leaves its cloud where it came to rest 2.5 s after the throw; an EMP mine set off by a walking character switches a camera 3 m away off; a frag mine set off by a walking character sets off a barrel 3 m away; a shot (a `damage.ballistic` ray) on a stuck jamming grenade starts a jamming field.
- **Not done here**: the shooting range lanes and the warehouse loot (M14 step 3).
- **Stick pose** (changed after the M14 playtest: grenades lay on walls with their base against the wall; Deus Ex stands them upright). `ThrowRules.stick_transform(point, normal, view, end_offset, side_offset)`: beside a surface steeper than `WALL_SLOPE_DEG` 45 degrees the body stands **upright** (+Y world up) with its side on the surface; on a floor (and a ramp up to 45 degrees) it stands on its base (+Y along the normal); on a ceiling it sits upside down on its base, so the blink light on its top cap faces the room (hanging by its top would bury the light in the ceiling). Its +X points along the view (projected on a floor or ceiling, straight into a wall), so the user sees its -X side: the lever in profile on the left and the canisters' pin ring in front. The centre sits where an upright cylinder of `Sticky.surface_offset` half height and `Sticky.side_offset` radius just touches the surface's plane (on a leaning wall, its rim). `side_offset`: 0.0405 m (`grenade.tscn`, the canisters: collision radius 0.04, model 0.038) and 0.046 m for the frag (its round body is 0.045 m wide; collision radius 0.04, a 6 mm collision gap). The ghost takes the same pose (`stick_spot()`). Tests: `stick_pose_test.gd` (each shipped grenade upright within 3 degrees beside a square, a turned and a leaning wall, its collision shape 0 to 1 cm from the surface; floor and ceiling; the ghost equals the stuck model's transform; a save keeps the pose), `throw_rules_test.gd`.

## As built (M14c: the range and the warehouse)

- **Grenade lane** (`levels/gallery/shooting_range/`, `GrenadeLane/`): behind the spray wall (its shadow keeps lane 5's shots off the table and the line; the dummies stay out of lane 4's line of fire). A throwing line 12 m from a 1.2 m target square (centre to centre); training dummies 2 m (due west), 4 m (due east) and 6 m (east, towards the line) from the target, centre to centre, each at least 1 m off the throw's axis so a rolling grenade passes them; a table with 5 of each grenade. **Measured**: Untrained, looking level from the line, a frag first touches down 0.21 m short of the target and **rolls on 3.75 m** before it goes off (the thrown-grenade roll M14a noted). Stuck on the target and shot from the line: 2 m dummy dead, 4 m alive with 0.7 of 80 torso, 6 m alive with 66.6 of 80 (a light wound). The frag is not "badly hurts" at 6 m: M14b's falloff stops that at about 5 m; not retuned (brief), left to the playtest. The table's sign says: for exact distances, stick one on the target, step back and shoot it.
- **Restock** (`objects/range/pickup_restock.gd`, `PickupRestock`, a Usable on a `range_button`): records every ItemPickup entity under its scope when it is ready (parent, name, scene, local transform, count, state) and on E makes the taken ones again (same name, so a save counts them as level-placed), puts back those moved more than 2 cm and refills partly taken ones. Any pickup, no per-item code. EventLog `range.restocked {by, count}`. It holds no state of its own (the layout comes from the scene), so it is not saveable.
- **Demolition on the range's tier switch**: grenades' `WeaponData.attribute_class` is `throw`, the class of the Demolition skill's `throw_distance`, so `WeaponSkill.skill_for()` (the tier switch, the tuning desk) and the Inventory tab's details name Demolition for a grenade in hand. No `throw_*` multiplier is written, so nothing else changes. A second "Skill tier" button and a "Reset dummies" button stand on the grenade table.
- **Mine corridor and EMP and jamming room**: an annex beyond the hall's east wall (the void east of hub z 28.5; the brief expected them inside the hall, but walls there would block the firing line's lanes and the long shots): a 6 x 3 x 1.6 m corridor (`Shell/MineCorridor`, shared walls, ADR 0043) through a 1.6 x 2.4 m opening, ending in a plain unlocked sliding door into a 10 x 4 x 10 m room (`Shell/EmpRoom`). In it (`EmpCorner/`): CAM-RANGE on the east wall, linked by Wi-Fi to AP-RANGE on the north wall (9.9 m), TURRET-RANGE with `range_turret.tres` (default preset `command.target_guards`: it never shoots the tester; a deck can retarget it), a powered ceiling lamp. Floor-plan distances: camera to turret 6.94 m, an EMP thrown under the camera takes it alone (measured by test); a jammer anywhere in the room jams the camera. No other electronic device within 12.5 m (the security wing's), none elsewhere in the range; the grenade lane is more than 15 m from every corner device.
- **Warehouse loot** (`tools/level_gen/warehouse_people.py`): frag on the security room's gun table, frag on the dock's metal supply crate (the cache shed spot was out of pick-up reach from its walk-in point), gas in the locker room's north open locker, EMP on the basement store shelf, jammer on the manager's desk. None on guards.
- **Tests**: `tests/levels/gallery/shooting_range/shooting_range_explosives_test.gd` (layout, restock, devices, distances, a jammer and an EMP in the room), `shooting_range_grenade_lane_test.gd` (the throw and the stuck frag above), `shooting_range_explosives_walk_test.gd` (a room fixture with its own navmesh: paths from the hub doorway to every area, the player walks through the corridor and its door; no other hub device in jamming reach), `tests/objects/range/range_controls_test.gd` (restock, the tier switch with a grenade), `tests/levels/mission/warehouse_people_test.gd` (the five grenades).
