# 20. Skills and augmentations list for v0

Status: Accepted (Electronics replaced by Computer: [ADR 0076](0076-computer-skill-multitool-removed.md))

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

A small but varied ability set is needed to exercise the framework.

## Decision

Skills with four tiers: Pistols, Low-Tech, Electronics, Lockpicking, Medicine. Augmentations: Cloak, Run Silent, Speed Enhancement, Combat Strength, Regeneration, powered by bioelectric energy and refilled by biocells.

## Alternatives considered

- Full Deus Ex list: unnecessary for v0.

## Consequences

- All augmentations usable at once in v0.
- See [abilities](../game/abilities.md).
