# 21. Mission area design

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

One mission must make systems combine through several routes.

## Decision

A small two-storey warehouse compound: steal a datapad from the security office and extract. Five guards, two cameras, one turret, one alarm panel, one breaker panel, four routes.

## Alternatives considered

- Several small missions.

## Consequences

- Scenario tests cover the routes.
- See [mission](../levels/mission-warehouse.md).
