# 24. Baton knockout rules

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Non-lethal play needs predictable rules.

## Decision

The baton deals damage.stun. One hit from behind on an unaware guard is a knockout; otherwise several hits, scaled by Low-Tech.

## Alternatives considered

- Always one-hit knockouts.

## Consequences

- Rewards stealth positioning.
