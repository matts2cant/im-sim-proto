# 117. Wireless as built: access points, radio links in the reach graph, the deck's Wi-Fi crack and join

Status: Accepted (builds [ADR 0115](0115-m12b-intrusion-and-m12c-consequences.md)'s "Wireless" and [ADR 0112](0112-hacking-reconciled-with-the-built-game.md#amendment-switches-routers-and-access-points-2026-11-11)'s access points; extends [ADR 0113](0113-network-layer-tracers-and-the-deck.md))

Date: 2026-11-11

## Context

ADR 0115 settled wireless for M12b: access points with a range, wireless links for any device but network gear, dashed light-blue links on the Map, an access point's Wi-Fi key cracked by aiming the deck at it within range (8 / 12 / 18 / 25 m by Computer skill) and in sight, the crack pausing when either is lost, and the deck joining the access point's subnet from anywhere in range. The approved mockup ([intrusion_screens.html](../prototypes/intrusion_screens.html): "Deck on the office wall jack" at Master, "Deck aimed at AP-YARD") shows the Map and the Wi-Fi key screen. This ADR records the details settled while building it.

## Decision

### Access points and radio links

- **`AccessPoint`** (`objects/network/access_point.gd`), a component on a device entity: `wireless_range` (m, centre to centre: entity origins; data, default 15), the Wi-Fi `key` and `key_strength` (`DeviceAccount.Strength`, the Icebreaker's base times), `client_address` (the address a deck joined over Wi-Fi gets; empty: its own address with the last number 200), who knows the key (saved). The shipped scene `objects/network/access_point.tscn` is a small box with two antennas, `Powered` and `Breakable` (health 30) like a switch, tagged `object.access_point`.
- **Network gear**: a device that carries traffic for others: tagged `object.network_gear` (the switch, router and access point scenes) or carrying a `Router` or `AccessPoint` component (`DeviceNetwork.is_network_gear()`). Gear never links wirelessly.
- **A wireless link is level data on the device**: `Device.wireless`, a path to the access point. `Device.wireless_link()` returns it only while the link holds: the target has an `AccessPoint`, this device is not network gear, and it stands within the access point's `wireless_range`. A link that cannot hold warns once after `_ready` (level data check). `Device.links` stay the cables.
- **Part of the reach graph**: `Device.graph()` links a device to its access point like a cable, so everything that follows reach follows: a broken or unpowered access point cuts its wireless devices off (their incidents reach no controller, screens show "offline" as for a cut cable) while a cabled device beside it stays up. A wireless device without its own subnet is on its access point's (`DeviceNetwork.subnet_of()` and the graph). A wireless device is not autonomous: no maintenance port.

### The deck's Wi-Fi

- **Range**: `DeckConfig.wireless_range` 8 m plus the Computer skill's new `deck_wireless_range` (ADD 0 / 4 / 10 / 17: 8 / 12 / 18 / 25 m; the Skills tab's row "Wi-Fi range"), straight line from the user's eye (`eye_transform()`, else the origin) to the access point's origin.
- **Aiming is a ranged tool target**: `ToolUse.step()` takes the entity aimed at within `aim_reach` (40 m; the Player casts one more ray, only while working a tool nothing within reach takes); a component with `accepts_ranged_tool(tool, user)` is worked through the same `tool_hold()`. `AccessPoint` accepts the deck within reach and from afar and hands it to the user's **`WirelessTool`** (a component beside `DeckSession`). The ray reaching the access point is the line of sight at that moment.
- **`WirelessTool.aim()`**: out of range, a notice ("AP-YARD: out of the deck's Wi-Fi range (14 m; yours 12 m)", the HUD's message line); in range with the key known, the deck joins at once and the screen opens on the Map; else the deck's screen opens on the **Wi-Fi key** view, plugged in or not (Map and Icebreaker greyed until the deck is on a network).
- **The crack** (`start()`, the screen's Crack the key / Resume / Stop): an `Icebreaker` job on the key (same base times, crack time multiplier and strongest strength as accounts), progress on the deck's stack (`state.wifi` {"<access point path>": {progress, hash}}; a changed key starts over). Each tick it advances only while the access point is up, in range, in sight (`has_sight()`: a ray from the eye to it, `PhysicsLayers.WORLD` only, the access point's own body ignored) and the would-be address is not blocked by a tracer; otherwise it **pauses** (still running, `pause_reason()`: out of range, line of sight lost, no signal, blocked by a tracer) and goes on by itself when all of that holds again. While it advances it adds trace like any tool (`Tracer.report_running()` on the access point: the tracers watching its subnet). It keeps running with the screen lowered; it stops (progress kept) when the deck leaves the inventory or the user cannot act.
- **Cracked**: the user knows the key (`AccessPoint.grant_key()`, saved) and the deck **joins** (`DeckSession.plug()` on an access point): a `NetworkSource` of kind `WIRELESS`, like a wall jack a graph node of its own (`Device.DECK_NODE`) linked to the access point, on its subnet, with its client address; the entry point (incidents of a full trace) is the access point. Discovery, scans, tools, logins and remote desktops work from it as from a jack. It stays joined, screen lowered or not, while the user's eye is within the deck's range of the access point; out of range it drops (`deck.unplugged` reason `out_of_range`) and its tools stop like a pulled plug.
- No Journal entry for a Wi-Fi key: knowing it is stored on the access point per user (the brief's "cracked keys"); a key found in a document is left for the levels brief if wanted.

### Screens

- **Wi-Fi key view** (`ui/deck/wifi_panel.gd`, the mockup's "Deck aimed at AP-YARD"): title bar "Aimed at AP-YARD · access point · 6.1 m" with the bars of the tracers watching the access point; "Wi-Fi key · AP-YARD", "key strength Weak · your range 12 m (Computer skill: Trained)", Signal (five bars falling with distance, "out of range" past the range) and the distance, Line of sight clear / blocked, the progress bar, Crack the key / Resume / Stop (keeps progress) ("too strong" greyed), "Paused: line of sight lost" (or out of range, no signal, blocked by a tracer) while a running crack waits, "Cannot crack: ..." beside a greyed button before it runs, the Trained time-left assist; cracked, "Key cracked: joined the SECURITY subnet over Wi-Fi (10.0.20.90) while in range" and a Map button.
- **Map**: `DeckScreen.cables_of()` includes wireless links; `NetworkGraph` finds them (`NetworkRow.wireless_cables()`: a cable whose two devices are radio-linked) and draws them dashed (6 px, gap 5) in `WIRELESS_COLOR` (#7fd4ff) while both ends are online, dashed grey otherwise. The layout is unchanged: wireless links are cables to it, so wireless devices hang on their access point (its comb when it has three links or more, else indented under it) and the tidy tree still has no crossing. An access point's node reads "AP-YARD · access point"; a wireless device's menu starts with "wireless via AP-YARD" (light blue). Joined over Wi-Fi, the title reads "Joined AP-YARD (Wi-Fi) · 10.0.20.90", the access point is the outlined entry point and the tools' note reads "Leaving the access point's range drops the Wi-Fi and stops its tools."

## Alternatives considered

- **A separate wireless graph or link flag in `DeviceGraph`**: nothing needs to tell a radio link from a cable yet (jamming in M14 may); the Map finds them from the devices.
- **Joining needs line of sight**: ADR 0115 says "from anywhere in range"; sight is the cracking challenge only.
- **The deck's range counting the access point's range too**: one range (the deck's, by skill) keeps the rule readable.
- **Aiming beyond range opening the screen with "out of range"**: a HUD notice says the same without an overlay the player cannot use.
- **The Wi-Fi crack inside `IcebreakerTool`**: its rules (network reach, stop on loss) differ from the key's (range and sight, pause); a sibling component keeps both small.

## Consequences

- `DeckSession.plug()` accepts an access point (join); `DeckSession.distance()`, `in_range()`, `wireless_range()`, `eye_of()` and `access_notice()` are static. `NetworkSource.has_own_node()` covers jacks and Wi-Fi.
- The network lab and the warehouse have no access points yet: a later level brief places them (tests use `network_fixtures.gd add_wireless()`: AP-YARD on the lab's security switch with a wireless camera and laser and a cabled camera beside it).
- Tests: `tests/objects/network/wireless_reach_test.gd` (reach through an access point, breaking and unpowering it, gear never wireless, the access point's range, an access point from components, the known key saved), `tests/items/deck/wireless_tool_test.gd` (range per tier, joining and dropping out of range, a wall pausing the crack, out of range pausing it, progress saved and resumed, too strong), `tests/ui/deck/deck_wifi_test.gd` (the Map's dashed links with 0 crossings, LMB aiming with the real Player, the Wi-Fi key screen through pause, crack and join), `tests/actors/common/abilities/skill_table_test.gd` (the Wi-Fi range row). Review shots: `tests/ui/deck/fixtures/wireless_shots.tscn` with `SHOT=map|crack|paused|joined|joined_map|world`.
