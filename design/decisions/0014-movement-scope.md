# 14. Movement scope: jump, mantle, vents

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Traversal options are needed for multi-route level design.

## Decision

v0 includes walk, crouch, sprint, jump, mantle and vents. Lean and ladders come later.

## Alternatives considered

- Include lean and ladders now.

## Consequences

- Lean and ladders are in the [backlog](../backlog.md).
- See [movement](../game/movement.md).
