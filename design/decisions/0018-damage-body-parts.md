# 18. Damage: Deus Ex 1 body parts and damage types

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Per-part injury creates tactical consequences.

## Decision

Use head, torso, two arms and two legs with per-part hitboxes, and damage types ballistic, blunt, stun, fire and explosive with resistances.

## Alternatives considered

- Single health pool.

## Consequences

- Per-part Health components and a HUD silhouette.
- See [damage](../game/damage.md).
