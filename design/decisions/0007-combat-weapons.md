# 7. Combat: one lethal, one non-lethal weapon

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

Immersive sims reward choice between lethal and non-lethal play.

## Decision

Provide a silenced pistol (lethal) and a stun baton (non-lethal).

## Alternatives considered

- Larger arsenal: unnecessary for v0.

## Consequences

- Both lethal and non-lethal approaches exist for the mission.
- See [weapons and aiming](../game/weapons-and-aiming.md).
